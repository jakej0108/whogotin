Privacy

Last updated July 2026.

If you create an account, we store your email address (or your name/email from Google, if you sign in that way). If you submit a result, we store whatever you choose to fill in — GPA, SAT/ACT, major, enrolling year, high school, essay summary, extracurriculars, and AP exams. All of that is optional beyond the required fields on the form, and it’s only ever what you typed in yourself.

You can share a result without making an account. Behind the scenes, we create an anonymous, passwordless session so the result has an owner in our database — this doesn’t collect any personal information from you, and it’s not tied to an email or password.

This is a crowd-sourced, public ledger — results you submit (GPA, major, school outcome, etc.) are visible to anyone who visits the site, by design. Your account email is never shown publicly. If you upload an admission letter or email to get verified, that file is stored privately — only the green “Verified” badge is shown to other visitors, never the document itself.

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don’t sell data to anyone. The only thing stored in your browser is your login session, so you stay signed in between visits.

Anyone can report a result that looks fake or wrong using the Report button on that result’s page. Reports are reviewed by hand, not by an automated system, and the reporter’s identity is never shared publicly.

Want a result taken down, or have a question about your data? Email whogotincontact@gmail.com.
